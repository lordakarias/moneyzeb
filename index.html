MoneyZeb – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: MoneyZeb keeps your money records on your phone. There is no account and no MoneyZeb server. We do not see, collect or sell your records. The app currently shows no ads.

1. Who we are

MoneyZeb is a personal money tracker for Android, made by TheKey (Zekarias Teshome), Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. In this policy, "we" means the developer of MoneyZeb.

2. The data you enter

Everything you type into MoneyZeb stays in a database on your phone. This includes:

  • income and expense records, categories and monthly plans;
  • bank and wallet amounts and their history;
  • loans, payments and the names and phone numbers of people you lend to or borrow from;
  • savings groups and their members;
  • shop products, sales and customers;
  • your shopping list and settings.

This data is not sent to us or to any MoneyZeb server. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it if it is lost. Names and phone numbers of other people are only stored on your phone, for your own records. Please only add people you know and who agree to it.

3. App PIN

If you turn on the PIN lock, the PIN itself is not saved. Only a protected (salted and hashed) version is stored on your phone, so the app can check it.

4. Backups and exports

You can export a backup file (JSON) or a spreadsheet file (CSV). These files are saved only where you choose, for example your phone storage or a cloud folder. Anyone who has the file can read it, so keep it in a safe place.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may also include the app's data in your personal Google account backup. This is controlled by Android and your Google account, not by MoneyZeb.

5. Ads

The current version of MoneyZeb shows no ads and shares no data with any advertising company.

To keep MoneyZeb free, a future version may show a small banner ad from Google AdMob. If that happens, we will update this policy before that version is released, explain what data the ad service may collect, and mention the change in the app's update notes. Your money records will never be shared with an ad service.

6. Permissions

MoneyZeb uses the internet only to check whether a newer version of the app is available. When you tap a phone number, MoneyZeb opens your phone's dialler; it does not make calls by itself. MoneyZeb does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or SMS.

7. Children

MoneyZeb is made for adults managing their own money. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

8. Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your phone, you control it. You can delete records inside the app, or remove all data by uninstalling MoneyZeb or using Settings → Apps → MoneyZeb → Storage → Clear data. Backup files you exported are not deleted automatically; delete them yourself if you no longer need them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
